# UPI Basics and UPI PIN Safety

## Source
National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI)

## Topic
UPI Basics, UPI PIN, Transaction Authorization

## UPI Basics

Unified Payments Interface (UPI) is an instant payment system developed by the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI), an RBI-regulated entity.

UPI allows users to make digital payments and transfer money using supported bank accounts and UPI applications.

## UPI PIN

The UPI PIN is a 4–6 digit passcode created by the user during UPI registration.

The UPI PIN is used to authorize bank transactions.

## UPI PIN Safety

Never share your UPI PIN with another person.

A user's UPI PIN should remain confidential.

Banks and legitimate customer-support representatives should not ask users to reveal their UPI PIN.

## Important Safety Rule

A UPI PIN is generally used when the user is authorizing a payment or other bank transaction.

Users should never enter their UPI PIN simply because someone asks them to do so over a phone call, message, or other communication.
