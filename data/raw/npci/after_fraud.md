# What To Do After UPI Fraud

## Source
National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI)

## Topic
Actions After Suspected UPI Fraud

## Act Quickly

If you believe that a fraudulent UPI transaction has occurred, act as quickly as possible.

## Contact Your Bank

Contact your bank through its official customer-support channel and report the suspicious or unauthorized transaction.

Do not use phone numbers or contact details received from an unknown person or suspicious message.

## Report the Fraud

Keep important transaction information available when reporting the incident.

Useful information may include:

- Transaction ID
- Transaction date and time
- Transaction amount
- UPI ID or recipient details
- Screenshots of relevant messages or transactions

## Protect Your Account

If you suspect that your banking credentials or authentication information has been compromised, follow your bank's official instructions to secure your account.

Never share your UPI PIN, OTP, password, or other confidential banking information while seeking help.

## Preserve Evidence

Keep relevant evidence such as:

- Transaction screenshots
- SMS messages
- Emails
- Phone numbers
- UPI IDs
- Payment references
- Other communication related to the incident

This information may be useful when reporting the incident to the bank or appropriate authorities.

## Important Reminder

Do not delay reporting a suspected fraudulent transaction.

Always use official banking and government channels when reporting financial fraud.
