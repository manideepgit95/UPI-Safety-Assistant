# UPI Fraud Awareness

## Source
National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI)

## Topic
Common UPI Fraud and Scam Patterns

## Common Fraud Patterns

UPI users may encounter different types of online scams and fraudulent activities.

Examples include:

- Fake cashback offers
- Fraudulent QR codes
- Fake customer-support contacts
- Suspicious links
- Requests to install unknown applications
- Fake investment schemes
- Fraudulent messages or social-media communications
- Requests for sensitive banking information

## Fake Cashback

Be cautious of unexpected messages or calls claiming that you have received a cashback or reward.

Do not follow suspicious links or provide confidential banking information simply to receive a supposed cashback.

## Fake Customer Support

Scammers may pretend to be customer-support representatives.

Do not share confidential information such as:

- UPI PIN
- OTP
- Passwords
- Card details
- Banking credentials

Use official customer-support channels instead.

## Unknown Applications

Be cautious if someone asks you to install an unknown application to solve a payment problem or receive money.

Unknown applications may create security risks.

## Suspicious Links

Avoid opening suspicious links received through unexpected messages, calls, or social-media communications.

Verify the source before interacting with a link.

## Important Reminder

If a transaction or request looks unexpected or suspicious, stop and verify the details before proceeding.
