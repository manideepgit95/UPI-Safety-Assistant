# UPI Payment and Collect Request Safety

## Source
National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI)

## Topic
UPI Payments, Collect Requests, and Transaction Safety

## UPI Payments

A UPI payment transfers money from the user's bank account to another account.

Before authorizing a payment, users should verify the recipient and transaction amount.

## Collect or Payment Requests

UPI applications may allow users to receive payment requests from another person.

A payment request should be reviewed carefully before it is approved.

## Safety Rules

Do not approve an unexpected payment request.

Before approving a request, check:

- Who sent the request
- The amount requested
- Why the payment is being requested
- Whether you actually expected the request

## Unexpected Requests

If an unknown person sends a payment request, do not approve it simply because they claim that the request is required to receive money.

If the request looks suspicious, reject it or use the appropriate reporting or blocking features provided by the UPI application.

## Important Reminder

Receiving money and making a payment are different actions.

Always check the transaction screen carefully before entering your UPI PIN or approving a transaction.
