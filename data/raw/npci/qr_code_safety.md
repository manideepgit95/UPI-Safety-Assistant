# UPI QR Code Safety

## Source
National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI)

## Topic
QR Code Safety and UPI Payments

## QR Codes and UPI

UPI QR codes can be used as part of UPI transactions.

Before completing a transaction, users should carefully check the payment details displayed by their UPI application.

## Important Safety Rule

Scanning a QR code and entering a UPI PIN is used to authorize a payment.

A user should not scan an unknown QR code or enter a UPI PIN simply because another person claims that it is necessary to receive money.

## Before Paying

Before entering the UPI PIN, check:

- The recipient's name
- The amount
- The transaction details
- Whether you actually intended to make the payment

## Unknown QR Codes

Be cautious when someone unexpectedly sends a QR code and asks you to scan it.

Do not enter your UPI PIN unless you have intentionally initiated and verified the payment.
