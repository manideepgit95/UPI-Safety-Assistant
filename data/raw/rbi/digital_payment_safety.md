# RBI Digital Payment Safety

## Source
Reserve Bank of India (RBI)

## Topic
Digital Payment Safety and Fraud Prevention

## Protect Sensitive Information

Never share confidential financial information with unknown people.

Sensitive information can include:

- UPI PIN
- OTP
- Passwords
- Card information
- Internet banking credentials
- Other authentication information

## Be Careful With Unknown Communications

Be cautious when receiving unexpected:

- Phone calls
- SMS messages
- Emails
- Links
- Websites
- Social-media messages

Do not provide financial information simply because someone asks for it.

## Suspicious Links and Websites

Avoid opening suspicious links received through unexpected messages or emails.

Fraudsters may use fake links and websites to obtain sensitive information.

Always verify that you are using the legitimate website or application of your bank or payment service.

## Unknown Applications

Do not install applications from unknown or untrusted sources simply because someone asks you to do so.

Fraudulent applications may attempt to access sensitive information stored on a device.

## Digital Payment Transactions

Exercise care when performing digital financial transactions.

Before authorizing a transaction, verify the transaction details and make sure you understand what you are approving.

## Important Reminder

If someone unexpectedly asks for your PIN, OTP, password, or other confidential banking information, do not share it.

Use official bank or payment-service channels when you need assistance.
