# Reporting UPI and Digital Payment Fraud

## Source
Reserve Bank of India (RBI) and Government of India Cyber Crime Reporting Portal

## Topic
Reporting Financial Cyber Fraud

## Act Quickly

If you become a victim of financial cyber fraud, report the incident as soon as possible.

## Contact Your Bank

Contact your bank through its official customer-care number or official banking channel and report the fraudulent transaction.

Follow the bank's instructions to protect the affected account and banking services.

## Cyber Crime Helpline

For immediate reporting of financial cyber fraud in India, call:

1930

The National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal identifies 1930 as the cyber financial fraud helpline. It is available 24x7.

## Online Reporting

Financial cyber fraud can also be reported through the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal:

https://www.cybercrime.gov.in/

## Information to Keep Ready

When reporting a financial cyber fraud, keep relevant information available, such as:

- Transaction ID
- Transaction date and time
- Transaction amount
- Bank or payment service details
- UPI ID or other transaction details
- Screenshots or other supporting evidence
- Relevant messages, phone numbers, or links

Providing accurate information can help authorities process the complaint.

## Important Reminder

Use only official bank and government channels when reporting fraud.

Do not contact unknown numbers or people who claim they can recover your money in exchange for a fee or confidential information.
