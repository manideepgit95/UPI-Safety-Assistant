# RBI QR Code Safety

## Source
Reserve Bank of India (RBI)

## Topic
QR Code and Digital Payment Safety

## QR Code Payments

QR codes can be used to make digital payments.

Before completing a QR-based payment, carefully check the transaction details displayed by the payment application.

## Verify Before Paying

Before entering your UPI PIN, verify:

- The recipient's name
- The amount
- The purpose of the payment
- Whether you intended to make the transaction

## Be Careful With Unknown QR Codes

Do not scan an unexpected or suspicious QR code simply because someone claims that it is necessary to receive money or obtain a refund.

Be especially careful when a QR code is received through an unexpected message, call, email, or social-media communication.

## UPI PIN Safety

Never share your UPI PIN with another person.

Do not enter your UPI PIN unless you have verified the transaction and intentionally want to authorize it.

## Important Reminder

Scanning a QR code can lead to a payment authorization flow.

Always check what transaction your payment application is asking you to authorize before entering your UPI PIN.
