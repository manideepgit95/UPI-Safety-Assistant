# National Cyber Crime Reporting

## Source
Government of India - National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal

## Topic
Cyber Crime Reporting and Financial Fraud

## Financial Cyber Fraud

If a person becomes a victim of financial cyber fraud, the incident should be reported as soon as possible.

Quick reporting can help initiate appropriate action.

## Cyber Crime Helpline

For reporting financial cyber fraud in India:

1930

The helpline is intended for reporting cyber financial fraud.

## Online Complaint

Cybercrime complaints can be submitted through the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal:

https://www.cybercrime.gov.in/

## Information to Keep Ready

When reporting a cyber financial fraud, keep relevant information available, such as:

- Transaction ID
- Transaction date and time
- Transaction amount
- Bank or payment-service details
- UPI ID or account details involved
- Screenshots
- SMS or email messages
- Phone numbers involved
- Other relevant evidence

## Preserve Evidence

Do not delete relevant messages, screenshots, transaction records, or other information related to the incident.

Such information may be useful when submitting a complaint.

## Use Official Channels

Use the official cybercrime portal and official bank channels when reporting fraud.

Be cautious of people who claim they can recover lost money in exchange for payment or confidential banking information.
